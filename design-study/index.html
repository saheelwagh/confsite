THE EDIT

Let the pattern speak. Let the content breathe.

My recommendation: the tile entrance once, a quiet timeline reveal, and subtle feedback on links. Keep glaze and line-drawing as optional accents. With motion off, every detail is already in place.

04 / NOTES FOR THE DESIGNER

A sense of place,
not a costume.

The strongest direction is azulejo as a visual grammar: repetition, framing, and relationships. Pair it with a contemporary, readable conference layout.

01

Pattern as punctuation

Concentrate ornament in one hero mural, thin dividers, and small marks. Leave paper guidelines and practical information on quiet backgrounds.

02

An editorial voice

A generous serif for headlines, a straightforward sans serif for details. Let long names and multilingual text shape the spacing.

03

A living place

Use original motifs and locally approved imagery. These illustrations are contemporary studies, not reproductions of historic tiles or buildings.
